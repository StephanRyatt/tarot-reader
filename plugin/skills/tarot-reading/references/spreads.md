# Spread library

Use these when the user names a spread or asks for a daily or weekly pull. Pass the `positions` array and the `label` exactly as written. Each position line gives its meaning for the reading.

## Card of the Day

For a single card that sets the tone of the day.

- positions: `["Card of the Day"]`
- label: `Card of the Day`

1. **Card of the Day** — The quality the day asks you to meet or embody.

## Daily Alignment

To support grounded presence and conscious choice through the day.

- positions: `["Day's Atmosphere", "Primary Focus", "Aligned Approach"]`
- label: `Daily Alignment`

1. **Day's Atmosphere** — The overall tone or quality shaping the day.
2. **Primary Focus** — What most deserves your attention or care today.
3. **Aligned Approach** — How to meet the day in a way that stays true to yourself.

## Daily Focus

For workdays or self-directed days, emphasising clarity, momentum and deliberate action.

- positions: `["Core Focus", "Mental Energy", "Challenge / Distraction", "Support / Ally", "Empowered Action"]`
- label: `Daily Focus`

1. **Core Focus** — The central theme to align with today.
2. **Mental Energy** — The mindset that best supports you today.
3. **Challenge / Distraction** — What could pull you off course or dilute your focus.
4. **Support / Ally** — A resource, strength or supportive condition available to you.
5. **Empowered Action** — One concrete action to take today, drawn from this card.

## Weekly Path

For practical clarity about the outer flow of the week: influences, challenges and direction.

- positions: `["Headline Energy", "Key Event / Influence", "Challenge", "Best Response", "Trajectory"]`
- label: `Weekly Path`

1. **Headline Energy** — The dominant outer atmosphere of the week.
2. **Key Event / Influence** — The kind of situation or influence likely to ask for your attention: a theme, not a specific event.
3. **Challenge** — The main tension or point of friction to work with.
4. **Best Response** — The choice, action or stance this week asks of you.
5. **Trajectory** — Where the week heads on its current course. It describes a direction you can still shape, not a fixed outcome.

## Weekly Evolution

For inner work: lessons, change and integration over the week.

- positions: `["Core Vibration", "Shadow / Old Pattern", "Soul Lesson", "Practice / Embodiment", "Emerging Self"]`
- label: `Weekly Evolution`

1. **Core Vibration** — The underlying psychological theme shaping the week.
2. **Shadow / Old Pattern** — An inherited habit, belief or reaction that asks to be seen and released.
3. **Soul Lesson** — The deeper teaching in the week's experiences.
4. **Practice / Embodiment** — A grounded practice or habit that supports integration.
5. **Emerging Self** — The quality or capacity coming into form.

## Past, Present, Future

The classic three-card spread, when the user asks for it by name.

- positions: `["Past", "Present", "Future"]`
- label: `Past, Present, Future`

1. **Past** — What led here and still shapes the situation.
2. **Present** — The heart of the situation now.
3. **Future** — Where this heads on its current course. A direction, not a fixed outcome.

## Decision

For a choice between two options. Replace `<A>` and `<B>` with short names for the options, such as "Stay" and "Go". Keep names, places and other private details out. For three or more options, add one position per option.

- positions: `["Heart of the Matter", "Option: <A>", "Option: <B>", "What You're Not Seeing", "What Matters Most in Choosing"]`
- label: `Decision`

1. **Heart of the Matter** — What this choice is really about.
2. **Option: \<A\>** — What choosing A asks of you, and where it leads on its current course.
3. **Option: \<B\>** — What choosing B asks of you, and where it leads on its current course.
4. **What You're Not Seeing** — The factor missing from how you're weighing it.
5. **What Matters Most in Choosing** — The value or need that should decide it.

## Celtic Cross

The traditional ten-card spread, for a full view of a complex situation. Use it only when asked.

- positions: `["Present", "Challenge", "Foundation", "Recent Past", "Crown", "Near Future", "Self", "Environment", "Hopes and Fears", "Where This Heads"]`
- label: `Celtic Cross`

1. **Present** — The heart of the situation now.
2. **Challenge** — What crosses it: the obstacle or tension.
3. **Foundation** — The root beneath the situation.
4. **Recent Past** — What is passing out of the situation.
5. **Crown** — The best that can be reached, or the conscious aim.
6. **Near Future** — What is coming into the situation next.
7. **Self** — How you stand in it.
8. **Environment** — The people and conditions around you.
9. **Hopes and Fears** — What you hope for and fear, often the same thing.
10. **Where This Heads** — Where the situation heads on its current course. A direction, not a fixed outcome.
